02

A cloud and a period.

The mark is a cloud drawn in one continuous line. The wordmark is Adscendo followed by a period.

Adscendo.

Primary lockup. Cloud on the left, wordmark in Geist 500 at −0.03em tracking. Use it whenever there is room.

Adscendo.

Positive. Ink #1d1d1f on white or mist.

Adscendo.

Negative. Mist #f5f5f7 on black or ink.

Icon. White cloud on a black square, 11/48 corners. Favicon, avatar and app.

Construction

The cloud sits in a 48 × 32 box with a 2.4 stroke, round caps and joins, and no fill. The cloud is 1.58× the wordmark's font size wide, and the gap between them is 0.42×. At 19px text (the size in the site's nav) that's a 30 × 20px cloud with an 8px gap.

Clear space

Keep a margin of half the cloud's height (0.5x) clear around the lockup. No text, edge or image goes inside it.

Minimum size: 80px wide on screen or 20mm in print. Below that, use the cloud alone (at least 16px).

03

No brand color.

Hierarchy comes from value, not hue. Color is saved for the clients' work.

Primary

Click a color to copy its HEX.

Supporting grays

Each gray has one fixed job. Use the darkest one the job allows.

Proportion

A typical page is almost all white and mist. Ink shows up in text and buttons; black takes one band at most.

Dark mode

Every token is a light/dark pair. Dark mode is not an inversion: each value was picked to hold contrast. Accent surfaces (the black band, the dark tile) take the scheme opposite the page, so in dark mode the black band turns light.

Weights in use

Regular400 · text, ledes
Medium500 · buttons, wordmark, labels
Semibold600 · every headline

Don't use 300, 700 or heavier. Don't use italics.

Secondary: the system stack

Adscendo has no second display font. The secondary font is the system font, used only when Geist isn't available: while the page loads, in email, Word, Google Docs and third-party decks.

Order of preference: SF Pro (Apple), Helvetica Neue, Arial. In office documents, use Arial at the weights and sizes of this scale. For code and technical data, a system mono (SF Mono, Menlo, Consolas).

font-family: "Geist", -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Helvetica Neue", Arial, sans-serif; <link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Geist:wght@400;500;600&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">

Composition rules

Two lines at most

Headlines fit in two lines on every screen, with text-wrap: balance. A long headline gets rewritten, not shrunk.

Headlines end in a period

Section headlines are statements: “How we work.” Project names and buttons take no period.

Two tones, display only

The hero headline puts the claim in ink and its complement in faint gray on the line below. Only in that role.

Sentence case

Only the first letter is capitalized, in headlines, buttons and menus. NO ALL CAPS.

No eyebrows

The headline opens the section. Don't put small labels above it. The one exception is the category on project cards.

Aligned figures

Prices and tables use tabular figures. The currency sign is a superscript at 0.32em.

05

Space separates. Lines don't.

Regions separate by a step in tone and generous space. Never by a hairline between sections, cards or list rows.

Grid

A 1080px container (the hero opens to 1240px) with fluid side margins of 20 to 48px.

Two-column grids are asymmetric: 1.15fr / 0.85fr for tiles, 0.9fr / 1.1fr for contact. They stack to one column between 820 and 860px. The project list uses three columns and stacks at 640px.

The hero and the before/after are centered. Everything else is left aligned.

Pills to act. Soft corners to hold.

Everything you click is a pill. Containers get corners that shrink with their size.

Only devices float

The interface is flat. Buttons, tiles, cards and the nav have no shadow. Shadows exist only on the device frames (browser and phone) that show the work, because a device is a physical object.

07

Pieces that repeat.

The components below are real: hover, click, type.

Buttons

  • Pill, 48px tall, 24px sides. Small: 32px and 14px.
  • Ink with white text. Hover goes to pure black; press scales to 0.98.
  • No border, no shadow. One primary button per block.

Chips

  • Designed for phones
  • Found on Google
  • Booking
  • Two languages
  • 14px pills, 8 × 14px padding.
  • Static labels: not clickable.
  • They sit at the foot of tiles, listing what's included.

Segmented control

  • Mist trough with 4px padding, 32px options.
  • Active option in ink with white text, 250ms transition.

Fields

  • White fill, 1px field line stroke, 12px corners.
  • Focus: ink stroke and a 3px halo at 12%. Error: red stroke.
  • Label above, 13px 500. Never placeholder alone.

Dark tile

The light tile's partner in a two-up grid. Ink ground, mist text, chips in #2C2C2E. 28px corners, 32 to 56px padding.

Theme

16 × 16px grid, 1.6 to 1.8 stroke, round caps and joins, colored with currentColor.

Motion

Calm, and only once. As a section enters the screen, its parts rise 24px and fade in over 900ms, 80ms apart (up to six steps).

Buttons and hovers: 200ms. Controls: 250ms. The nav's light/dark switch: 300ms.

House curve: cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1). With reduced motion on, everything appears still.

09

The work shows up running.

No stock photos. Adscendo shows real product, inside a device.

Screenshots

From the live product or the project's own review captures, always with sample data. WebP, 16:10 on desktop, 780 × 1688 on phones.

Illustrations

Illustrated sites carry a small note saying they're illustrative and not client work. The “before” may use dated fonts and colors; that stays inside the illustration.

Sharing

Social image as a 1200px JPEG twin of each project's cover. Icon: white cloud on the black square. Founder photo cropped to a circle.

12

Do's and don'ts.

The summary to check before publishing anything.

Do

  • Keep it monochrome: ink, white, mist and the grays.
  • Use one black band per page at most, at the key moment.
  • Separate regions with tone steps and 88 to 160px of space.
  • Set headlines in Geist 600, negative tracking, two lines at most, with a period.
  • Make buttons, chips and controls pills.
  • Show the work inside a device and say when it's an illustration.
  • Respect the mark's clear space and minimum size.

Don't

  • Create an accent color for links, buttons or focus.
  • Draw hairlines between sections, cards or list rows.
  • Put a shadow on anything that isn't a device.
  • Use a second font, italics, all caps or positive tracking.
  • Put eyebrows above section headlines.
  • Make up testimonials or imply partnerships with brands.
  • Color, distort or drop the period from the wordmark.
