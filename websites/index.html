Questions about websites.

How much does a website cost?

A new website built from scratch costs $800, or $400 with our 50% launch discount. A new version of an existing website costs $1,200 ($840 at launch), and a website with integrations like online booking, payments or a CRM costs $2,000 ($1,900 at launch). The final price comes after a closer look at your project.

I already have a website. Do I need a new one?

Not always. If your site is slow, hard to use on a phone or doesn't bring in customers, a new version usually pays off, and it costs less than starting from scratch because we reuse what already works: $840 at launch. Send us the address and we'll tell you honestly what we'd do.

Will my business show up on Google and in ChatGPT?

We build every site to be found: fast pages, clear organization, your details in a format Google and AI understand, and words written the way your customers ask. Nobody can honestly guarantee first place, so we show you where you stand and what is improving.

Do we need to meet in person?

No. Adscendo works 100% remotely: we talk by video call, send updates by email or WhatsApp, and you try each version on your own phone and computer before it goes live.

Who will build my website?

Jean Menezes, Adscendo's founder, works with you directly. Before founding Adscendo in October 2026, Jean spent years designing and building digital products for large companies, with projects for McDonald's, AT&T, Petronas, PagBank and Multilaser.
