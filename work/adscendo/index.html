01

A name that climbs.

Adscendo is Latin for “I rise.” To move upward, to climb. The mark is a single line-drawn cloud, and the tagline says what the work is for: from complexity to forward motion.

The visual rule is just as short. White and black, simple, in the spirit of Apple's product pages. The only color on the page belongs to the work it shows.

02

Why a studio, and why now.

Jean spent years on digital projects for companies like McDonald's, AT&T, Petronas, PagBank and Multilaser. In 2026 he started building his own products, Sidemed first, and took on client work like Minha companhia.

Adscendo gives that work a name and a home. Born in Brazil, built to operate globally.

03

Day one: the system before the site.

Adscendo was founded on October 27, 2026, and the first thing built was not a website. It was Prospector, an AI prospecting system: it finds businesses on the map, analyzes each one's website, writes a personal email, and follows every lead in a CRM.

It has one golden rule. No email goes out without explicit approval.

04

Day two: live.

On October 28 the first site went live. Its signature was a demonstration instead of a promise: as you scroll, a dated website turns into a modern one inside a browser frame.

This portfolio grew out of it, keeping the same design language and telling the story of each project, in English and Portuguese.
