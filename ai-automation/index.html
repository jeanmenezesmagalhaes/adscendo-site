What is AI automation?

AI automation is software that does repetitive work for you and uses AI for the parts that used to need a person to read, write or decide: sorting emails, pulling data out of documents, drafting replies, qualifying leads.

Classic automation, including RPA (robotic process automation), follows fixed rules: if this happens, do that. AI automation also handles messy inputs, like a free-text email or a scanned invoice, and hands off to a person when it isn't sure.

What an AI automation agency does

An agency like ours does the whole job, not just the tool:

  • Maps how the work happens today and where the time goes.
  • Picks which tasks are worth automating first, and says which ones aren't.
  • Builds the workflows and connects them to your email, spreadsheets, CRM, WhatsApp or business software, where they allow it.
  • Tests them on real cases from your business before they go live.
  • Trains your team and fine-tunes everything in the weeks after launch.

Examples of AI workflow automation for small businesses

  • New leads. An inquiry arrives, AI reads it, scores it, writes a first reply and adds it to your CRM.
  • Customer questions. An AI agent answers common questions on your website, by email or on WhatsApp, and passes the rest to your team. More in our guide to AI agents for business.
  • Documents. Invoices, orders and forms read automatically and entered into your system.
  • Reports. Numbers pulled from several tools into one weekly summary, without anyone copying and pasting.
  • Sales follow-up. Reminders and personalized emails sent at the right time. Our own Prospector finds businesses and writes the first email this way.

Which tasks are worth automating?

A task is a good candidate when it shows most of these signs:

  • It happens many times a week.
  • It's mostly copying, sorting or answering the same things.
  • A mistake is easy to spot and fix.
  • It takes real hours from people who could be selling or serving customers.

Not worth it yet: tasks that happen once a month, or decisions that need careful judgment every time. Start where the payoff is clear, and expand from there.

How we work

The same four steps as our AI consulting: an assessment of how the work happens today, a plan with what to automate first and what it costs, the build with tests on real cases, and training with follow-up once it's live.

What AI automation costs

It depends on how many workflows you need, which tools they connect to and how much data they handle, so there's no price list. After a free first conversation, we send a proposal with the scope and the price before any work starts.

AI tools also charge a monthly fee or per use. The proposal includes the expected running cost, so there are no surprises later.

Questions about AI automation.

What's the difference between AI automation and RPA?

RPA repeats fixed steps, like a person clicking through the same screens. AI automation can also read and write: it understands an email, extracts data from a document or drafts a reply. Many good workflows combine both.

Do I need to change my software?

Usually not. We connect automations to the tools you already use, where they allow it, and only suggest a change when a tool really gets in the way.

Will automation replace my team?

The goal is to take repetitive work off your team's plate so they have more time for customers and sales. Where AI isn't sure, a person stays in charge.

How long until an automation is live?

It depends on the workflow. The plan you get after the assessment has a timeline for each one, before anything gets built.

Is my business data safe?

We decide with you which data each automation can use, give it access only to what it needs, and choose tools that fit the privacy rules your business follows.

Do you work outside the US?

Yes. We work remotely with businesses in the United States and Brazil, in English or Portuguese.
