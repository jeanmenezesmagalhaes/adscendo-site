Questions about apps.

iPhone, Android or web: which do I need?

If your customers will use it often and on the go, an app in the stores makes sense. If they'll use it now and then, or at a desk, a web app is faster to launch and needs no download. We recommend what fits on the first call.

Do you publish it on the App Store and Google Play?

Yes. We prepare everything both stores ask for and send the app for review, so it reaches your customers' phones.

Do I need a website too?

Usually, yes. The website is where people find you on Google and in AI answers, and where they learn about the app before downloading it. We can build both in one project.

Have an app in mind?

Tell us what it should do and who it's for. We reply within 6 hours, and the first call is free.
