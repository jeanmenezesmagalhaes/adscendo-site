Websites / Website cost guide

How much does a small business website cost?

Usually from a few hundred to several thousand dollars to build, plus a small yearly cost to keep it online. Where you land depends on who builds it and what the site has to do. Here is how the numbers work, with our own prices at the end.

Updated November 10, 2026 · By Jean Menezes, founder of Adscendo

How much does a website cost, by who builds it?

Who builds the site moves the price more than anything else:

  • You, on a website builder like Wix or Squarespace: roughly $200 to $600 a year in plan fees, plus the hours you put in.
  • A freelancer: often $1,000 to $5,000, depending on their experience and how much they write and design for you.
  • A small studio: often $1,000 to $10,000. Ours run from $400 to $1,900 at launch prices.
  • An agency: often $5,000 to $30,000 or more, with a team of designers, writers and developers.

These are typical ranges in the US, not quotes. Two sites at the same price can include very different work, so compare what's included, not only the total.

What changes the price of a website?

Five things decide most of what a website costs:

  • How many pages. A one-page site for a local service costs less than a site with a page for each service, location and team member.
  • Who writes the text and finds the photos. If you hand over finished text and photos, the job is smaller. If the designer writes it, it takes longer, but it's usually written better for Google.
  • Custom design or a template. A template is cheaper and faster. A custom design fits your brand and is built around what your customers need to do.
  • What the site has to do. Online booking, payments, a store or a connection to your CRM each add work, and each one has to be tested.
  • How many languages. Each language is close to a second site: its own text, its own pages and its own address on Google.

What does a website redesign cost?

A redesign usually costs a bit less than a new site from the same designer, because your text, photos and pages already exist. It costs about the same when the site needs new pages, new features or a full rewrite.

A good redesign keeps your address and sends old pages to the new ones, so what you've built on Google carries over.

Ours is $840 at launch prices ($1,200 regular), or $1,900 if the site connects to booking, payments or a CRM.

What does a website cost every year?

The build is paid once. A few smaller costs come back every year:

  • Domain (your address, like yourbusiness.com): about $10 to $20 a year.
  • Hosting (the server that keeps the site online): from free to about $30 a month for a small business site. Website builders include it in the plan.
  • Email at your domain, if you want it: billed per person, per month, by Google or Microsoft.
  • Changes after launch: you make them yourself, or pay your designer by the hour or by the month.

Before you sign with anyone, ask what the site will cost per month once it's live. A cheap build with an expensive monthly plan can cost more after two or three years.

Questions to ask before you hire anyone

  • Who owns the site and the domain once it's paid for?
  • What will it cost per month after launch, and what does that include?
  • Who writes the text, and is it written with the words customers type into Google?
  • Can I change text and photos myself?
  • Can I see sites you've built, live?

The answers tell you more than the price does.

Questions about website cost.

How much does a basic website cost for a small business?

A simple site with a few pages usually costs $1,000 to $5,000 from a freelancer or a small studio, or a few hundred dollars a year if you build it yourself on a website builder. Ours starts at $400 with the launch discount ($800 regular).

Is a website builder cheaper than hiring a designer?

At first, yes. Over a few years the plan fees add up, and the hours you spend building are hours away from your business. A builder makes sense to test an idea. A designer makes sense when the site has to bring in customers.

Why do some websites cost $500 and others $20,000?

Mostly because of the work behind them: how many pages, who writes the text, custom design or a template, and features like booking, payments or a store. Agencies also have bigger teams to pay. Compare what's included, not only the price.

Do I pay once or every month?

For our websites, once: one price for the whole project. The domain and hosting are separate yearly costs, usually small. Ask us and we'll tell you what they would be for your site.
