Questions

Do I need to understand AI?

No. You only make the introduction. We explain, run the diagnosis and build it. The materials show what AI can do, with real examples.

How much can I earn?

15% of every contract that closes, for every business you refer. For example, a website with integrations costs $1,900 at our launch prices, so that one referral pays you $285.

When do I get paid?

After the client pays. If they pay in installments, you get 15% of each one.

How do you know the referral is mine?

Send us the contact before the client reaches out, or ask them to mention your name. We confirm every referral by email.

Who talks to the client?

We do. You make the introduction; we handle the conversation, the diagnosis, the proposal and the work.

Do websites and apps count?

Yes. If the business you referred needs a website or an app, the same 15% applies.

Does it cost anything to join?

No. You fill in the form and we email you the materials.

Is this an affiliate program?

Not quite. There are no links or cookies to track: you introduce a business to us directly, and you earn 15% when its contract closes.
