What we do.

We start where the payoff comes fastest, and keep going until AI is part of the everyday work.

Assessment

We learn how your business runs today and show you where AI saves time or money. And where it doesn't.

Less repetitive work

Copying data from one system to another, building spreadsheets and reports, answering the same questions over and over. If your team does it on autopilot, software can do it.

AI agents for customer service

An assistant that answers common questions on your website, by text or by email, at any hour, and hands off to someone on your team when it should.

AI in sales and the back office

Qualifying leads, writing the first message, organizing orders and paperwork. It's the kind of work our own Prospector does in sales.

Works with what you already use

AI works alongside your spreadsheets, email, phone and business software, where they allow it. No need to replace everything at once.

Team training

Your team learns the tools with examples from your own business, not a generic course.

How it works.

From the first assessment to follow-up, in four steps.

  1. 01

    Assessment

    We talk with you and with the people who do the work, look at your processes and software, and list where AI can help.

  2. 02

    Plan

    You get a plan: what to do first, what each change solves and what it costs, before anything gets built.

  3. 03

    Build

    We build the automations and agents, connect them to your systems and test them on real cases from your business before they go live.

  4. 04

    Follow-up

    We train your team, look at what changed in the daily routine and fine-tune whatever needs it in the weeks that follow.

Questions about AI consulting.

Do I need to be technical to hire you?

No. You explain how your business runs and where the work gets stuck. We handle the technical side, and your team learns only what it will use day to day.

My business is small. Does this make sense?

Often, yes: in a small business, a few hours less of repetitive work each week already makes a difference. The assessment tells you honestly whether it's worth it and where to start.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of your business and what we build, so there's no price list. After a first conversation, which is free, we send you a proposal with what's included and what it costs, before any work starts.

Will AI replace my team?

The idea is to take repetitive work off your team's plate, so they have more time for what needs a person: taking care of customers, selling and solving problems. Where AI falls short, a person stays in charge.

What about my business data?

We decide with you which data AI can use, give each automation access only to what it needs, and choose tools that fit the privacy rules your business has to follow.

Where should your business start?

Tell us what your business does and where the work gets stuck the most. Jean replies personally.
