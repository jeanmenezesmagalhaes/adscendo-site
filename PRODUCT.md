# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS generated by a small Node script (`src/build.mjs`) from `src/content.mjs`. No framework. Deploy target: Vercel static hosting.

## Users

Owners of small and mid-size businesses deciding whether Adscendo can make them more visible and win them more customers, plus partners and peers who want to see what the studio has shipped. The copy is written for the business owner first: simple, outcome-first, no jargon. Many read on a phone. English speakers land on `/`; Brazilians often prefer `/pt/`.

## Product Purpose

Adscendo is the consultancy of Jean Menezes. This site is his portfolio under the Adscendo name: it tells the story of each product he has created or built (how it was conceived, the decisions behind it, and the UI as shipped), and ends in a contact. Success = a conversation about a new project.

## Positioning

Brazilian warmth, American standards: the studio is Brazilian and says so, as an asset for US clients (creativity and a human, close way of working, delivered to the standard they expect). Shown on the home page between the work list and the timeline.

A new studio with a senior founder: Jean has worked on digital projects for McDonald's, AT&T, Petronas, PagBank and Multilaser, and now offers that level of craft to local businesses. Born in Brazil, built to operate globally.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: websites first, apps second (internal systems are not offered on the site for now, decided 2026-10-29).
- Process shown on the home page: listen (meeting to understand the business), build (first version), launch (adjust and put live for the client).
- Contact is a short scripted chat (no AI): name, website yes/no (+ link and integrations), a first estimate, email, optional WhatsApp and notes, then one "Send" that emails the summary through FormSubmit, with a promise of a reply within 6 hours then one "Message us on WhatsApp" button for whoever wants a faster reply (the same single button, with the summary, is the fallback if sending fails). Estimate tiers (USD, regular price → launch discount): from scratch $800 → 50% off ($400); new version of an existing site $1,200 → 30% off ($840); site with integrations $2,000 → 5% off ($1,900). "Estimate only. Final price after a closer look at your project." The email step offers common provider shortcuts (EN: gmail, icloud, outlook; PT: gmail, hotmail, outlook).
- Contact channels: form (service endpoint pending, e.g. Formspree), email jeanmenezesmagalhaes@icloud.com, WhatsApp +55 51 99327-1160, LinkedIn https://www.linkedin.com/in/jean-magalhaes/, Instagram https://www.instagram.com/adscendo.consult/.
- Two languages: US English (primary, `/`) and Brazilian Portuguese (`/pt/`), kept in parity.

## Product Principles

1. Honesty over hype: new studio, experienced founder, real price.
2. The site is the proof: its own craft must outclass what the visitor has now.
3. Show, then tell: every story pairs the decisions with the real UI.
4. One clear next step: form, email, or WhatsApp.
