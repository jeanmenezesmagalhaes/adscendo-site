# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS (single page, no framework). Deploy target: Vercel or Netlify static hosting.

## Users

Owners and managers of US small and mid-size businesses (dentists, med spas, clinics, independent real estate teams, plumbers, roofing contractors, independent restaurants). They arrive from a personalized cold email that pointed out concrete problems with their current website. They are busy, skeptical of agencies, read on a phone between appointments or jobs, and are deciding in under a minute whether this studio is real and worth replying to.

## Product Purpose

Adscendo is a boutique studio founded by Jean Menezes that builds beautiful websites and internal systems. This site exists to make the cold email credible: prove the studio is real, show taste through its own craft, explain the portfolio offer, and get the visitor to reply, send the form, or message on WhatsApp. Success = a contact (form, email, or WhatsApp) from a US business owner.

## Positioning

A new studio with a senior founder: Jean has worked on digital projects for McDonald's, AT&T, Petronas, PagBank and Multilaser, and now offers that level of craft to local businesses. Born in Brazil, built to operate globally.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: presentation websites (responsive, SEO + GEO optimized for Google and AI search, social-ready) and internal systems.
- Portfolio offer: first 10 US clients get a $1,200 website for $400. Delivery about one week.
- Process: discovery (call + form) → delivery for review → adjustments if needed.
- Contact channels: form (service endpoint pending, e.g. Formspree), email jeanmenezesmagalhaes@icloud.com, WhatsApp (number pending), LinkedIn https://www.linkedin.com/in/jean-magalhaes/, Instagram https://www.instagram.com/adscendo.consult/.
- Copy in US English.

1. Honesty over hype: new studio, experienced founder, real price.
2. The site is the proof: its own craft must outclass what the visitor has now.
