How AI picks who to recommend.

ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity read the web, then name a few businesses. To be one of them, your site has to pass three tests.

  1. 01

    Read

    AI tools visit websites to read them, a bit like a person would. If your site turns them away or is hard to read, they move on to the next one.

  2. 02

    Trust

    They check that you are real: the same name, address and phone everywhere, reviews, and who runs the business.

  3. 03

    Quote

    They pick sentences that answer the question directly. A site that answers what your customers ask, in plain words, gets quoted.

Why it comes with a new website.

Most small business sites were made before anyone asked AI for a recommendation. They tend to fail in the same three ways.

Closed to AI

Many sites turn AI tools away without the owner knowing, because of a setting nobody remembers turning on.

Hard to read

On many sites the important words are inside pictures, behind pop-ups or slow to appear. People can see them. AI often can't.

Nothing to quote

The site never says clearly what you do, where and for whom, and doesn't answer the questions customers really ask.

Patching an old site piece by piece usually costs more and works worse. So we build the new one with AI optimization inside from day one.

Questions about AI optimization.

What is AI optimization?

It's the work that makes your website easy for AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity to read, trust and recommend. Being found on Google gets you on a list of links. Being recommended by AI gets your name in the answer. You may also see it called GEO.

Why do you sell it with a new website?

Because most of what AI needs is built into the site itself: how the pages load, how they are organized and what they say. Fixing that on an old site usually costs more and works worse than building a new one the right way, so we do both in one project, for one price.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend my business?

No, and be careful with anyone who does. AI answers change with each question, each person and each day. We guarantee that AI can read, understand and trust your site, and we show you where you stand before and after launch.

How long until AI starts mentioning my business?

It varies. AI tools that look things up online as they answer, like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI answers, can find a new site within a few weeks. What they already "know" changes more slowly, over months. We ask the same questions at launch and again later, so you see what changed.

Does this replace being found on Google?

No, they work together. AI tools use Google and Bing to find websites, so every site we build is set up for both.

What is the free AI check?

We ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity the questions your customers ask, look at how your website is built and send you one page with what we found. It is free, and you don't have to hire us.

Do you keep track after launch?

If you want. We can repeat the AI check every month and send you a short report: where your business appeared, who appeared instead and what we changed. Ask about it when we talk.

My website is new and I like it. Can you do only the AI part?

Sometimes. If your site is recent and well built, we can add AI optimization to it. Send us the address and we'll tell you honestly whether it's worth it.
