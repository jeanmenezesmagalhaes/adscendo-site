Questions about websites.

I already have a website. Do I need a new one?

Not always. If your site is slow, hard to use on a phone or doesn't bring in customers, a new version usually pays off, and we reuse what already works. Send us the address and we'll tell you honestly what we'd do.

How much does a website cost?

Ours run from $400 to $1,900 at launch prices, one price for the whole project. Elsewhere it ranges from a few hundred dollars a year on a website builder to $30,000 or more at an agency. Our website cost guide explains what changes the price and what you pay every year.

How much does a website redesign cost?

Ours is $840 at launch prices ($1,200 regular), because we start from what your current site already has. If it connects to booking, payments or a CRM, it's $1,900. Our website cost guide explains what changes the price.

Why are your prices lower than most agencies'?

Because we're a new studio, and our first clients get launch discounts of up to 50%. We're also small and remote, so there's no office or account manager built into the price. You work directly with the person who designs and builds your site.

How long does it take?

Usually 2 to 3 weeks for a new site, from the first call to launch, once we have your text and photos. Sites with booking, payments or a CRM take a little longer. You get a date before we start.

Who owns the website and the domain?

You do. The domain is registered in your name, and once the project is paid, the site is yours. If you ever move on, we hand everything over.

Can I change text and photos after launch?

Yes. Send us the change by email or WhatsApp and we make it for you. Changes after launch are billed per change. Besides that, you only pay the domain and hosting, usually small yearly costs.

Will my business show up on Google and in ChatGPT?

We build every site to be found: fast pages, clear organization, your details in a format Google and AI understand, and words written the way your customers ask. Nobody can honestly guarantee first place, so we show you where you stand and what is improving.
