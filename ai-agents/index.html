AI agents for business: what they do and how to start.

An AI agent doesn't just answer questions. It takes steps: books the appointment, updates the record, sends the quote. Here is what agents can do in a small business, where they fall short and how to start without wasting money.

Updated November 11, 2026 · By Jean Menezes, founder of Adscendo

Talk to usWhat they can do

What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is software that uses a language model, like the ones behind ChatGPT, to work toward a goal. It reads a request, decides the next step, uses the tools you allow (a calendar, your CRM, a database, email) and acts, then reports back or hands off to a person.

A chatbot answers. An agent answers and does something about it.

What AI agents can do in a small business

  • Customer service. Answer common questions on your website, by email or on WhatsApp, at any hour, and hand off to your team when a person is needed.
  • Scheduling. Find a free slot, book it and send the confirmation and reminders.
  • Sales. Qualify new leads, write the first message and follow up at the right time.
  • Back office. Read invoices, orders and forms and enter the data into your system.
  • Internal help. Answer your team's questions using your own documents and procedures.

AI agent, chatbot or automation?

  • Chatbot: answers questions, usually from a script or a knowledge base. It doesn't act in your systems.
  • Automation: runs fixed steps when something happens. Reliable, but it doesn't understand messy requests. See AI automation.
  • AI agent: understands the request, chooses among the actions you allowed and carries them out. Most useful when requests vary but the possible actions are clear.

Off-the-shelf platform or custom AI agent?

Ready-made platforms work well for simple cases, like answering questions from a help page. A custom agent makes sense when it has to connect to your own systems, follow your business rules or handle a process that's specific to you.

Either way, the hard part isn't the AI model. It's deciding what the agent may do, giving it the right information and testing it on real cases.

How to start without wasting money

  • Pick one task with real volume, like the questions your team answers every day.
  • Write down what the agent can and can't do, and when it must call a person.
  • Give it access only to the data that task needs.
  • Test it on real past cases before customers see it.
  • Launch with a person reviewing, then measure: time saved, questions solved, handoffs.

Risks to plan for

Language models can give confident wrong answers. Good agents limit what they talk about, cite your own information, and pass anything uncertain to a person. Tell customers they're talking to an assistant, and keep sensitive data out of tools that don't need it.

Questions about AI agents.

How much does an AI agent for business cost?

Platforms charge a monthly fee, often by usage. A custom agent is a project plus the running cost of the AI tools it uses. We send a proposal with both after a free first conversation.

Can an AI agent work on WhatsApp?

Yes, through the official WhatsApp Business Platform from Meta. Tools that imitate a regular phone number risk getting it banned.

Will my customers know they're talking to AI?

They should. Telling them builds trust, and a good agent hands off to a person quickly when it's needed.

Do I need technical staff to run an agent?

No. Once it's live, your team reviews conversations and adjusts answers in plain language. We handle the technical side.
