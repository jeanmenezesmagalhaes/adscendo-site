Minha companhia

A marketplace where families book verified professionals to accompany their parents.

Product design and build
When
October 2026
Platform
Web, phone and desktop

Live, in validation
minhacompanhia.com.br

01

A brief for screens only.

The client wanted a platform where families hire someone to accompany an elderly mother or father on a task: a doctor's appointment, an exam, the bank, the market, a walk.

The first contract was deliberately small. Screens only, no working system yet, to validate the presentation, the content and the order of the hiring flow before building anything. The working name was Filha de Aluguel, Portuguese for “daughter for hire.”

02

Designed for the daughter.

The person who decides is usually the daughter. She is busy, often far away, and deciding on her phone. On October 20 the prototype was tested with a user, and the verdict was clear: the home page has to say at once that this is a marketplace where she picks and hires a professional.

The search came out of that session working for real, with filters by city, task, time and price that live in the URL.

03

Words carry weight.

In Portuguese, the obvious word for a companion, acompanhante, also means escort. So it is never used. The service is “accompanying on tasks,” and the person is “a verified professional.”

The product got a new name to match the tone: Minha companhia, “my company,” as in someone keeping you company. Warm, respectful, and never talking down to the older person.

04

Trust, built into the flow.

When a stranger accompanies your mother, trust can't be a badge. It has to be in every step.

  • Monthly verificationDocuments are reviewed every month, with the last and next review dates on the profile.
  • Honest quotesHourly rate, minimum hours, and whether a car is included, all visible before hiring.
  • Blind reviews, both waysFamily and professional rate each other, and neither sees the other's score until both have rated or 14 days pass.
  • Payment held until the reviewThe money only goes to the professional after the family rates the visit.

05

From prototype to product.

Within a week, the static prototype gained a real backend: accounts, service requests, two-way reviews and social sign-in. Each professional has a public portfolio she can share as her own page.

Minha companhia is live at minhacompanhia.com.br, covering all of Brazil, while the rest of the flow is validated.
