Scope: site/index.html, the Adscendo marketing one-pager. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: US small-business owners arriving from a cold email, mostly on phones, skeptical, deciding in under a minute.
Action: request a free homepage concept (hero form: current website + email), or contact by form, email, WhatsApp.
Proof on hand: founder experience (McDonald's, AT&T, Petronas, PagBank, Multilaser, as plain text, no logos), portfolio names (URLs pending). No testimonials; none invented.
Constraints: user-pinned world (white and black, Apple-like simplicity, cloud logo as main mark). Code-led (no image generation). Concept-seed not run: direction pinned by user.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page proves the craft instead of claiming it: a dated local-business website turns into a beautiful one as you scroll. It refuses the agency default of a headline over three service cards and a logo wall.
OWN-WORLD: Apple product-page grammar. White ground, #f5f5f7 bands, one full-black band for the price. Ink #1d1d1f, gray #6e6e73, hairlines #d2d2d7. Geist set large and tight. Black pill buttons. Line-drawn cloud mark. No accent color: the only color on the page lives inside the illustrative client site.
STORY: The visitor sees their own problem (old site, shrunken on a phone, no booking), watches it fixed, learns the honest offer ($400 instead of $1,200 for the first 10 US clients, about one week), trusts the founder's track record, and sends their site URL.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Centered: cloud + "Adscendo." nav, headline at about 5.5rem in two lines, one sentence of subcopy, an inline two-field form (current website, email) with a black "Get a free concept" pill. The top of the device frame for the before/after rises into the bottom of the viewport.
FORM: Apple product page with a sticky scroll-driven reveal, position 1 (pinned). Signature interaction: a clip-path wipe from "Before" to "After" tied to scroll, with a browser frame on desktop and a phone frame on mobile. seed: none (skipped: direction pinned by the user; concept-seed not run)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
